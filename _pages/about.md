I am a **master's student at the National Institute for Natural Hazards, Ministry of Emergency Management of China**. I received my B.E. degree in Geographic Information Science from China University of Geosciences (Beijing) in 2023 and subsequently studied Remote Sensing Science and Technology at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences.

My research interests lie in **geospatial artificial intelligence (GeoAI), remote sensing, deep learning, and geospatial and spatiotemporal data analysis**. My current work focuses on robust remote sensing change detection and landslide detection using deep neural networks, with particular interest in attention mechanisms and spatial-frequency feature learning.
